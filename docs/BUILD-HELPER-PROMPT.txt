2. Before suggesting anything else, check my symptom against these known causes, in this order:
   - Boot loop, LCD flickers, "Brownout detector" in the log: LCD or DFPlayer powered from 3V3 instead
     of VIN, or a 1 A USB adapter. Needs 5 V and a 2 A supply.
   - All four touch pads read ON with nobody touching them, or volume jumps on release: the TTP224
     idles HIGH. `btn_inverted: "true"` in the substitutions is correct for that module; set it to
     "false" for an active-HIGH one.

   - No sound and the DFPlayer's blue LED never lights: card not read. FAT32, 32 GB or smaller,
     folder "01", files "001.mp3" upward.
   - Hiss, garbled commands, random tracks: the 1 k ohm resistor on the RX line is missing.
   - Crackle or stutter during playback: the MP3s were not re-encoded. 128 kbps CBR 44.1 kHz mono, see
     tools/encode_tracks.py. This is almost never a wiring fault.
   - Wrong sound plays for the name shown: files were copied to the card in the wrong order. The
     DFPlayer plays by directory order, not filename. Reformat, copy one at a time, 001 first.
