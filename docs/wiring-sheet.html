Read this first

  1. Pin positions on the drawing are schematic. Find each pin by its printed label; GND appears several times on an ESP32 and any one is fine.
  2. LCD backpack runs on 5 V (VIN). At 3.3 V the contrast is too faint to read.
  3. Arcade button: microswitch COM to GND, NO to GPIO32, NC empty. Any GND pin. Internal pull-up handles it.
  4. TTP224 V goes to 3V3 so its outputs stay at 3.3 V. GPIO34/35 are input-only pins, which is fine for this module. Pad 1 goes to GPIO27 (spare input, no job yet). Our modules idle HIGH and pull LOW on touch; firmware is set for that. If every pad reads ON untouched on a new module, flip btn_inverted in that board's YAML.
  5. DFPlayer runs on 5 V. Its TX at 3.3 V logic is safe into GPIO16. The 1k on the RX line is required: without it the player hisses and can misread commands.
  6. Speaker goes straight to SPK_1 / SPK_2. The player has its own 3 W amp, so no resistor, diode or MOSFET module.
  7. Use a 2 A USB adapter. The player draws bursts near 1 A at full volume.
  8. Piezo is a passive buzzer between GPIO25 and GND, for pad-press blips only. Leave it off if you don't have one.
  9. Wait about 2 s after power before expecting the player to respond; the firmware already does.

Parts per clock

  • ESP32 WROOM dev board, 30 pin, USB-C + data cable
  • 16x2 LCD with PCF8574 I2C backpack
  • Arcade button, Sanwa OBSF-24 (microswitch COM / NO / NC)
  • TTP224 4-pad touch module (all four pads wired, pad 1 spare)
  • DFPlayer Mini, YX5200 chip
  • microSD card, 32 GB or less, FAT32, folder 01 with 001.mp3–010.mp3 (Track 1–10 in HA)
  • Speaker, 32 mm full-range, 8 Ω 3 W
  • 1 kΩ resistor for the RX line
  • Passive piezo (optional)
  • 5 V USB wall adapter, 2 A
  • Dupont wires: 4 LCD, 2 button, 5 touch, 5 DFPlayer, 2 speaker, 2 piezo

Bench checks with bench.yaml

  • Power up from a USB port. LCD backlight on, ESP LED on, DFPlayer LED off until it plays.
  • I2C scan in the log shows the backpack. If it says 0x3F, change address in both YAMLs.
  • LCD prints "Bench OK" and a counting uptime. Blank boxes only: turn the contrast pot on the backpack.
  • Arcade button plays /01/001.mp3; the player's blue LED lights while playing. Pad 2 stops it.
  • Pads 3 / 4 step volume down / up while a track plays.
  • Silent, LED never lights: card not read. Reformat FAT32, check folder/file names, reseat the card.
  • Hiss or garbled commands: the 1k on RX is missing, or TX/RX are swapped.
  • Log shows "DFPlayer finished track" after a song ends. That event is what loops the alarm.
  • Then flash alarm-board-1 and press Test Alarm in HA.
