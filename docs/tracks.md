# Kids alarm clock tracks

Copy the ten MP3s into a folder named `01` on a FAT32 microSD (32 GB or less), in this order on an empty card.
The "Alarm Track" selector in Home Assistant lists them by name; position N plays `/01/0NN.mp3`.

| File | HA name | Sound | Source / licence |
|------|---------|-------|------------------|
| 001.mp3 | Rooster | Rooster crowing in the morning, 4 s | Mixkit #2462, Mixkit License |
| 002.mp3 | Clock Alarm | Morning clock alarm, 9 s | Mixkit #1003 |
| 003.mp3 | Birds | Little birds singing in the trees, 8 s | Mixkit #17 |
| 004.mp3 | Classic Alarm | Classic alarm, 5 s | Mixkit #995 |
| 005.mp3 | Digital Buzzer | Digital clock alarm buzzer, 8 s | Mixkit #992 |
| 006.mp3 | Farm Animals | Farm animals in the morning, 44 s | Mixkit #7 |
| 007.mp3 | Morning Joy | Morning Joy [ALARM CLOCK], 33 s | Pixabay #20961, Lesiakower, Pixabay Content License |
| 008.mp3 | Chiptune | Chiptune [ALARM CLOCK], 62 s | Pixabay #112869, Lesiakower |
| 009.mp3 | Soft Plucks | Soft Plucks [ALARM CLOCK], 60 s | Pixabay #120696, Lesiakower |
| 010.mp3 | Be Happy | Be Happy (Wake Up Now), song | Pixabay #130700, AlexGrohl |

Short clips loop on the clock until snoozed or dismissed.
